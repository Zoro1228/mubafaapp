What is Mubafa?

Mubafa helps you see where your money goes, in plain words. Add money you spent and money you earned, see your monthly balance, and keep your home budget simple. It is made for anyone at home to use every day, not just for experts.

You can use it in any currency, and in English + 4 other languages. Your entries stay on your own phone. The app has no internet permission, so it works fully offline.

Features

Money in and money out

Add spending and earnings quickly, and see them by day and by month.

Monthly balance

See your opening balance, totals, and closing balance for each month.

Where money went

See spending by category, so you know what took the most money.

Budget

Set a monthly budget and see how much of it is left.

Savings goal

Set a goal and watch your savings grow towards it.

Market list

Keep a shopping list at the bottom of Home, tick items off as you buy them.

Udharo

Track money you gave and money you took, with totals to collect and to repay.

Bills due

Keep bills in one place and see what is due.

Medicines

Keep a simple list of medicines.

Repeating entries

Set entries that repeat, so you do not have to type them every time.

PDF monthly statement + CSV

Download a professional monthly statement as a PDF, or export your entries as a CSV spreadsheet file.

Notes and calculator

Keep short notes and use the built-in calculator.

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android above. The app file (1.2 MB) saves to your phone’s Downloads.
  2. Tap the downloaded Mubafa.apk file. If asked, allow “install from this source” for your browser — this is normal for apps outside the Play Store.
  3. Open Mubafa and start adding your first entry. No account and no sign-in are needed.

Your data stays on your phone. Mubafa has no internet permission and does not send your entries anywhere.
