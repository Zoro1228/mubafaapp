# Mubafa - Simple Finance Tracker for Android

Mubafa is a simple finance tracker for Android, made by Faisal M. Khan for his own family and a special person in Nepal.

It is free for everyone, works offline, and needs no accounts and no internet. It works in English + 4 other languages, and in any currency.

What it does:
- Money in and money out
- Monthly balance, and where money went
- Budget, savings goal, and market list
- Udharo to collect and to repay
- Bills due, medicines, and repeating entries
- PDF monthly statement and CSV export
- Notes and calculator

Download:
- Website: https://zoro1228.github.io/mubafaapp/
- Android app: download Mubafa.apk from the website, or from the Releases section of this repository. Android 8.0 and newer.

How to install:
1. Download Mubafa.apk
2. Open it and allow install from this file if your phone asks
3. Open Mubafa and start adding your money in and out
